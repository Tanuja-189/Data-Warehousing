# Need to understand the business context and ownership by asking these kind of questions

1. Who owns the data?
2. What process does it supports?
3. What are the source file formats?
4. What is the data model/catalog?
5. Any documentation from System and Data?
6. What are the integration capabilities (any API, Kafka, Direct extracts like that)
7. In what form and in what database the data is stored..?
8. What kind of loads are needed?
9. What is the data scope and historical needs?
10. What is the expected size of the data?
11. Any limitations to extracts..?
12. Any other aspects which can impact the performance of the source systems?
13. Any authentications to the source systems?
14. What is the frequency of the data?

-- Understand the structure of the incoming data by seeing source data or ask any technical person regarding it.
